Where do I begin?+

Start with a brief description of what you would like to discuss. You do not need to have everything organized before asking about a consultation.

What should I prepare for a consultation?+

Note your questions and priorities. In a real engagement, the office would explain what information is needed and how to share it securely. Do not send personal documents through this demo.

Can I ask about fees before moving forward?+

Yes. A consultation is an opportunity to ask about the proposed work, fees, and next steps before deciding whether to proceed. This concept does not quote legal fees or offer consultations.
